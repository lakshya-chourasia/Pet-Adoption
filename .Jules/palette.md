## 2024-03-12 - Accessible Icon Buttons in Cards
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons within card components need contextual aria-labels and titles specific to the card's content (e.g., "Add [Pet Name] to favorites") to maintain accessibility and usability for screen readers and sighted users without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always verify that icon-only buttons have descriptive aria-labels and title attributes dynamically populated based on adjacent context.
