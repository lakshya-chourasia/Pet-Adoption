## 2024-05-22 - [Context-Aware ARIA Labels]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like "favorite" hearts) often lack context. Generic labels like "Favorite" are okay, but specific labels like "Add Monti to favorites" allow screen reader users to act without needing to navigate around to find context.
**Action:** Always include the item name in the ARIA label for actions within a list/card view.
