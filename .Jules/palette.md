## 2024-05-18 - Missing Accessible Names on Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** The project uses an icon-only button pattern for pet "Favorite" actions within cards. These buttons lacked `aria-label` and `title` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and difficult to understand for users who rely on tooltips. We also learned the app's components should prefer `:focus-visible` over `:focus` to reduce visual noise for mouse users while keeping keyboard navigation clear.
**Action:** Applied a script to dynamically add `aria-label="Favorite [Pet Name]"` and corresponding `title` attributes to all icon buttons based on the associated pet's name. Changed `.icon-button:focus` to `.icon-button:focus-visible` in the CSS. For future components, always ensure icon-only buttons include descriptive accessible names and prefer `:focus-visible` for focus states.
